# Warfarin
Warfarin is a drug based on the active ingredient Warfarin Sodium, belonging to the category of Anticoagulants and specifically Vitamin K antagonists.
## What Warfarin is used for
Warfarin is used to prevent and treat blood clots in conditions such as atrial fibrillation, deep vein thrombosis, and pulmonary embolism, and to reduce the risk of stroke in patients with certain heart conditions.
## How to use Warfarin
- Adults: dose individualized based on regular INR monitoring, typically starting at 5-10 mg once daily and adjusted to maintain a target INR range of 2.0-3.0, taken at the same time each day.
## When Warfarin should not be used
- hypersensitivity to the active ingredient or to any of the excipients;
- active or recent significant bleeding, or high bleeding risk conditions;
- high risk of falls, such as in patients with Senile Dementia, Mild Alzheimer's Dementia, or recent Fractured Femur, due to the danger of serious bleeding from trauma;
- severe hepatic or renal impairment;
- pregnancy.
