# Amlodipine
Amlodipine is a drug based on the active ingredient Amlodipine Besylate, belonging to the category of Cardiovascular agents and specifically Dihydropyridine Calcium channel blockers. It is often used as an alternative to Beta-blockers such as Propranolol in patients with asthma or other bronchospastic conditions.
## What Amlodipine is used for
Amlodipine is used to treat Hypertension and chronic stable angina by relaxing and widening blood vessels, lowering blood pressure and reducing cardiac workload without affecting the airways.
## How to use Amlodipine
- Adults: 5 mg once daily, which may be increased to a maximum of 10 mg once daily depending on blood pressure response, with or without food.
## When Amlodipine should not be used
- hypersensitivity to the active ingredient, other dihydropyridines, or to any of the excipients;
- severe hypotension;
- unstable angina or cardiogenic shock;
- significant aortic stenosis.
