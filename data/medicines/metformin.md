# Metformin
Metformin is a drug based on the active ingredient Metformin Hydrochloride, belonging to the category of Antidiabetic agents and specifically Biguanides.
## What Metformin is used for
Metformin is used as first-line treatment to lower blood glucose levels in patients with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus, particularly those who are overweight or obese, as it also supports modest weight reduction and does not typically cause hypoglycemia on its own.
## How to use Metformin
- Adults: 500-850 mg 2-3 times a day with meals, gradually titrated up to a maximum of 2550-3000 mg per day depending on tolerance and glycemic control.
## When Metformin should not be used
- hypersensitivity to the active ingredient or to any of the excipients;
- moderate to severe renal insufficiency, due to increased risk of lactic acidosis;
- severe hepatic insufficiency;
- acute conditions with risk of altered renal function such as dehydration, severe infection, or shock;
- conditions of tissue hypoxia such as decompensated heart failure or recent myocardial infarction.
