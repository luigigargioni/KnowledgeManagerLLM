# Rivaroxaban
Rivaroxaban is a drug based on the active ingredient Rivaroxaban, belonging to the category of Anticoagulants and specifically Direct Factor Xa inhibitors (DOACs). It is often preferred over Warfarin since it does not require regular INR monitoring, though bleeding risk in falls remains a concern.
## What Rivaroxaban is used for
Rivaroxaban is used to prevent stroke in patients with atrial fibrillation, and to prevent or treat deep vein thrombosis and pulmonary embolism, offering a more predictable dosing regimen than Warfarin.
## How to use Rivaroxaban
- Adults: 20 mg once daily with the evening meal for stroke prevention in atrial fibrillation, or as otherwise directed depending on indication.
## When Rivaroxaban should not be used
- hypersensitivity to the active ingredient or to any of the excipients;
- active clinically significant bleeding;
- severe renal impairment (creatinine clearance below 15 mL/min);
- hepatic disease associated with coagulopathy;
- pregnancy and breastfeeding.
