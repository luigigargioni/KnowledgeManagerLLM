# Memantine
Memantine is a drug based on the active ingredient Memantine Hydrochloride, belonging to the category of Anti-dementia agents and specifically NMDA receptor antagonists. It is often used as an alternative or add-on to Donepezil, particularly in moderate to severe dementia, with dose adjustment required in renal impairment.
## What Memantine is used for
Memantine is used to slow the progression of cognitive symptoms in moderate to severe Alzheimer's disease or dementia by regulating glutamate activity in the brain, helping to preserve memory and daily functioning for longer.
## How to use Memantine
- Adults: 5 mg once daily initially, increased gradually in weekly increments to a maintenance dose of 20 mg once daily, with dose reduction in patients with moderate renal impairment.
## When Memantine should not be used
- hypersensitivity to the active ingredient or to any of the excipients;
- severe renal impairment without dose adjustment;
- history of seizures, due to a lowered seizure threshold.
