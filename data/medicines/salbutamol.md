# Salbutamol
Salbutamol is a drug based on the active ingredient Salbutamol Sulfate, belonging to the category of Bronchodilators and specifically Short-acting Beta-2 agonists (SABA).
## What Salbutamol is used for
Salbutamol is used for the rapid relief of acute asthma symptoms such as wheezing, shortness of breath, and chest tightness, as well as prevention of exercise-induced bronchoconstriction in patients with conditions such as Mild Asthma.
## How to use Salbutamol
- Adults: 1-2 inhalations (100-200 mcg) as needed for acute symptom relief, up to a maximum of 8 inhalations per day. Can also be used 10-15 minutes before exercise to prevent exertion-induced symptoms.
## When Salbutamol should not be used
- hypersensitivity to the active ingredient or to any of the excipients;
- history of serious tachyarrhythmias;
- caution required in patients with uncontrolled hyperthyroidism or severe cardiovascular disease.
