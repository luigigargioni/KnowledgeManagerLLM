# Propranolol
Propranolol is a drug based on the active ingredient Propranolol Hydrochloride, belonging to the category of Cardiovascular agents and specifically Non-selective Beta-blockers.
## What Propranolol is used for
Propranolol is used to treat Hypertension, angina, certain arrhythmias, and to reduce heart rate and cardiac workload; it is also used for migraine prevention and control of physical symptoms of anxiety, such as tremor and palpitations.
## How to use Propranolol
- Adults: 40 mg 2-3 times a day, adjusted according to response, up to a maximum of 320 mg per day for hypertension, taken before meals.
## When Propranolol should not be used
- hypersensitivity to the active ingredient or to any of the excipients;
- bronchial asthma or history of bronchospasm, including Mild Asthma, due to risk of triggering severe airway constriction;
- severe bradycardia, sick sinus syndrome, or second/third-degree atrioventricular block;
- decompensated heart failure;
- severe peripheral arterial circulatory disturbances.
