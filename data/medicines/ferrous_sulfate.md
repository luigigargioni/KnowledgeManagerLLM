# Ferrous Sulfate
Ferrous Sulfate is a drug based on the active ingredient Ferrous Sulfate, belonging to the category of Antianemic preparations and specifically Iron supplements. It is used to treat Iron-Deficiency Anemia, though its absorption may be reduced by concurrent use of Proton Pump Inhibitors such as Omeprazole.
## What Ferrous Sulfate is used for
Ferrous Sulfate is used to prevent and treat iron deficiency and Iron-Deficiency Anemia by replenishing the body's iron stores, which are necessary for the production of healthy red blood cells.
## How to use Ferrous Sulfate
- Adults: 100-200 mg of elemental iron per day, divided into 1-3 doses, ideally taken on an empty stomach with water or juice rich in Vitamin C to enhance absorption; may be taken with food if gastrointestinal discomfort occurs.
## When Ferrous Sulfate should not be used
- hypersensitivity to the active ingredient or to any of the excipients;
- hemochromatosis or other iron overload disorders;
- hemolytic anemia not caused by iron deficiency;
- active peptic ulcer disease or inflammatory bowel disease, due to risk of gastrointestinal irritation.
