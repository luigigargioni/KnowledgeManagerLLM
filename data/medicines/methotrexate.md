# Methotrexate
Methotrexate is a drug based on the active ingredient Methotrexate, belonging to the category of Disease-Modifying Antirheumatic Drugs (DMARDs) and specifically Folic acid antagonists.
## What Methotrexate is used for
Methotrexate is used to reduce inflammation, pain, and joint damage in patients with Rheumatoid Arthritis by suppressing an overactive immune system, and can help slow the progression of the disease over time.
## How to use Methotrexate
- Adults: 7.5-25 mg once weekly (not daily), taken orally or by injection on the same day each week, generally combined with folic acid supplementation on non-Methotrexate days to reduce side effects.
## When Methotrexate should not be used
- hypersensitivity to the active ingredient or to any of the excipients;
- severe renal or hepatic impairment;
- pregnancy and breastfeeding;
- active infections or significant immunodeficiency;
- pre-existing blood disorders such as severe anemia, since Methotrexate can further suppress bone marrow function.
