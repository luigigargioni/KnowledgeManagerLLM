# Lisinopril
Lisinopril is a drug based on the active ingredient Lisinopril, belonging to the category of Cardiovascular agents and specifically ACE inhibitors (Angiotensin-Converting Enzyme inhibitors).
## What Lisinopril is used for
Lisinopril is used to treat Hypertension and Heart Failure, and to improve survival after a heart attack, by relaxing blood vessels and reducing the workload on the heart.
## How to use Lisinopril
- Adults: 10 mg once daily initially, adjusted according to blood pressure response up to a maximum of 40 mg once daily, taken at the same time each day with or without food.
## When Lisinopril should not be used
- hypersensitivity to the active ingredient or to any other ACE inhibitor;
- history of angioedema related to previous ACE inhibitor therapy;
- pregnancy;
- bilateral renal artery stenosis;
- caution required in patients with Chronic Kidney Disease due to risk of hyperkalemia and worsening renal function, requiring close monitoring.
