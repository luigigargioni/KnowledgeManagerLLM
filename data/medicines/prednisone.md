# Prednisone
Prednisone is a drug based on the active ingredient Prednisone, belonging to the category of Corticosteroids and specifically Glucocorticoids. It is used for conditions such as Rheumatoid Arthritis and severe asthma exacerbations.
## What Prednisone is used for
Prednisone is used to reduce inflammation and suppress an overactive immune response in conditions such as Rheumatoid Arthritis flares, severe allergic reactions, and asthma exacerbations, providing rapid relief of swelling and pain.
## How to use Prednisone
- Adults: 5-60 mg once daily depending on the condition being treated, generally taken in the morning with food to reduce gastric irritation, with dose tapered gradually rather than stopped abruptly after prolonged use.
## When Prednisone should not be used
- hypersensitivity to the active ingredient or to any of the excipients;
- systemic fungal infections;
- uncontrolled Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus, since corticosteroids raise blood glucose levels;
- osteoporosis or recent fractures, such as a Fractured Femur, due to further weakening of bone density;
- active untreated infections.
