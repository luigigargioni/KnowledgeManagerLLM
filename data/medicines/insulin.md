# Insulin
Insulin is a drug based on the active ingredient Human Insulin (or insulin analogues), belonging to the category of Antidiabetic agents. It is often used as an alternative to oral agents such as Metformin in patients with significant renal impairment, since it does not carry the risk of lactic acidosis.
## What Insulin is used for
Insulin is used to control blood glucose levels in patients with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus, particularly when oral medications are insufficient, contraindicated, or unsuitable due to reduced kidney function.
## How to use Insulin
- Adults: dose individualized based on blood glucose monitoring, body weight, and diet, typically administered by subcutaneous injection once to multiple times daily according to the prescribed regimen (basal, bolus, or mixed).
## When Insulin should not be used
- hypersensitivity to the active ingredient or to any of the excipients;
- during episodes of hypoglycemia;
- caution required in patients with cognitive impairment such as Senile Dementia or Mild Alzheimer's Dementia, due to increased risk of dosing errors and unrecognized hypoglycemia without proper supervision.
