# Ibuprofen
Ibuprofen is a drug based on the active ingredient Ibuprofen, belonging to the category of Analgesics, Antipyretics and Non-Steroidal Anti-Inflammatory Drugs (NSAIDs) and specifically Propionic acid derivatives.
## What Ibuprofen is used for
Ibuprofen is used to relieve mild to moderate pain (such as headache, muscle pain, or joint pain), reduce fever, and control inflammation, including flare-ups of conditions such as Rheumatoid Arthritis.
## How to use Ibuprofen
- Adults: 200-400 mg every 4-6 hours as needed, not exceeding 1200 mg per day unless otherwise directed by a physician. Take with food or milk to reduce gastrointestinal irritation.
## When Ibuprofen should not be used
- hypersensitivity to the active ingredient, other NSAIDs, aspirin, or any of the excipients;
- severe renal or hepatic insufficiency;
- active gastrointestinal ulcer or history of recurrent ulceration/bleeding;
- severe heart failure, since NSAIDs can cause fluid retention and worsen cardiac decompensation;
- third trimester of pregnancy.
