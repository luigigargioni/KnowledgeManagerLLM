# Donepezil
Donepezil is a drug based on the active ingredient Donepezil Hydrochloride, belonging to the category of Anti-dementia agents and specifically Acetylcholinesterase inhibitors.
## What Donepezil is used for
Donepezil is used to temporarily improve memory, thinking, and daily functioning in patients with mild to moderate Alzheimer's disease or Senile Dementia by increasing levels of a brain chemical involved in memory and learning.
## How to use Donepezil
- Adults: 5 mg once daily at bedtime, which may be increased to 10 mg once daily after 4-6 weeks depending on tolerability and clinical response.
## When Donepezil should not be used
- hypersensitivity to the active ingredient, piperidine derivatives, or to any of the excipients;
- severe bradycardia or sick sinus syndrome;
- active peptic ulcer disease;
- severe hepatic impairment.
