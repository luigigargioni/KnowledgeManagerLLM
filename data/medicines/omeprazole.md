# Omeprazole
Omeprazole is a drug based on the active ingredient Omeprazole, belonging to the category of Antiulcer agents and specifically Proton Pump Inhibitors (PPIs). It is commonly prescribed alongside NSAIDs such as Aspirin or Ibuprofen to protect the stomach lining.
## What Omeprazole is used for
Omeprazole is used to reduce stomach acid production, treating conditions such as gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD), peptic ulcers, and gastritis, and to prevent gastric damage in patients taking long-term NSAID or Aspirin therapy.
## How to use Omeprazole
- Adults: 20 mg once daily in the morning before breakfast, for a duration depending on the underlying condition, up to a maximum of 40 mg once daily for severe cases.
## When Omeprazole should not be used
- hypersensitivity to the active ingredient or to any of the excipients;
- concomitant use with certain antiviral drugs (e.g. nelfinavir) due to reduced efficacy;
- caution in long-term use, since it may reduce absorption of iron and vitamin B12, and increase fracture risk with prolonged high-dose therapy.
