# Atorvastatin
Atorvastatin is a drug based on the active ingredient Atorvastatin Calcium, belonging to the category of Lipid-lowering agents and specifically HMG-CoA reductase inhibitors (statins).
## What Atorvastatin is used for
Atorvastatin is used to lower elevated cholesterol and triglyceride levels and to reduce the risk of cardiovascular events such as heart attack and stroke, particularly in patients with Hypercholesterolemia, Ischemic Heart Disease, or other cardiovascular risk factors.
## How to use Atorvastatin
- Adults: 10-20 mg once daily, up to a maximum of 80 mg once daily depending on cholesterol targets, taken at any time of day with or without food.
## When Atorvastatin should not be used
- hypersensitivity to the active ingredient or to any of the excipients;
- active liver disease or unexplained persistent elevations of liver enzymes;
- pregnancy and breastfeeding;
- concomitant use with certain antifungal or antiviral drugs that increase the risk of muscle toxicity (myopathy/rhabdomyolysis).
