# Furosemide
Furosemide is a drug based on the active ingredient Furosemide, belonging to the category of Diuretics and specifically Loop diuretics.
## What Furosemide is used for
Furosemide is used to reduce fluid overload and swelling (edema) associated with Heart Failure, kidney disease, or liver disease, and to help control blood pressure by promoting the elimination of excess salt and water through urine.
## How to use Furosemide
- Adults: 20-40 mg once daily in the morning, adjusted according to clinical response, up to a maximum of 80-120 mg per day in divided doses for severe fluid overload, taken with food to reduce gastric irritation.
## When Furosemide should not be used
- hypersensitivity to the active ingredient, other sulfonamides, or to any of the excipients;
- anuria or complete renal failure unresponsive to diuretics;
- severe electrolyte depletion (hyponatremia, hypokalemia) or dehydration;
- hepatic coma or pre-comatose states associated with liver cirrhosis.
