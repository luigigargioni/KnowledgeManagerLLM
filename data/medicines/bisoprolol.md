# Bisoprolol
Bisoprolol is a drug based on the active ingredient Bisoprolol Fumarate, belonging to the category of Cardiovascular agents and specifically Cardioselective (beta-1 selective) Beta-blockers.
## What Bisoprolol is used for
Bisoprolol is used to treat Hypertension, chronic stable angina, palpitations and stable chronic heart failure; it lowers heart rate and cardiac workload.
## How to use Bisoprolol
- Adults: 2.5-5 mg once a day, adjusted according to response, up to a maximum of 10 mg per day, taken in the morning after breakfast.
## When Bisoprolol should not be used
- hypersensitivity to the active ingredient or to any of the excipients;
- acute or decompensated heart failure, cardiogenic shock;
- severe bradycardia, sick sinus syndrome, or second/third-degree atrioventricular block;
- untreated pheochromocytoma;
- severe metabolic acidosis;
- severe bronchial asthma or severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.
## Cautions and monitoring
- Mild Asthma: Bisoprolol is the most beta-1 selective agent of its class and is not contraindicated in mild, well-controlled disease, unlike non-selective beta-blockers such as Propranolol. Selectivity is still dose-dependent: start at 2.5 mg, keep a rescue bronchodilator available, and monitor for wheeze after the dose.
- Diabetes: may mask the warning signs of hypoglycaemia, in particular tachycardia and tremor.
- Chronic Kidney Disease: reduce the dose in severe renal impairment.
- Never stop the treatment abruptly: withdrawal can cause rebound angina or arrhythmia.
